# Grants from the Generalitat de Catalunya / ACCIÓ / FEDER Catalunya for Synkiria's "Physical AI" humanoid robot project (status at 18-oct-2026)

Method note: research was done on 18-oct-2026. The primary sources were tramits.gencat.cat procedure pages (status, dates, DOGC references), ACCIÓ official fact sheets ("fitxes informatives", PDF) and the ACCIÓ aid listing page. I could not fetch the full DOGC text of any 2026 bases: the DOGC portal loads its content with JavaScript and the PDF service returned HTTP 503. Every "article/base" citation for equipment eligibility is therefore missing and is listed as a gap. Where I give eligibility rules, they come from the official ACCIÓ fact sheet for that call, not from the article text of the bases. Third-party sites (tecnociminnova, aitpa/texfor, CIT-UPC, Compàs) were used only as leads and are labelled when cited.

## Q1. Which relevant calls are OPEN on 18-oct-2026?

### Takeaway
On 18-oct-2026 ACCIÓ lists only two open aid calls: "Cupons d'estratègia de creixement 2026" and "Cupons de programes europeus d'R+D+I 2026", both until 16-nov-2026 at 14:00. Both pay 100% of an advisory service from an ACCIÓ-accredited provider. Neither funds hardware or a robot. The strategy coupon also excludes micro-enterprises outright. I found no open Generalitat/ACCIÓ cash grant on that date that would pay for buying a Unitree R1.

### Inferences
- **Assessment:** Synkiria is very probably a micro-enterprise (turnover about €290k, small team). If so, it cannot use the strategy coupon. Even if it could, the coupon pays only consultancy, not the robot.
- **Assessment:** The European R+D+I coupon is the only open line that could plausibly fit. It would help only if Synkiria wanted to join a Horizon Europe or Digital Europe consortium on embodied AI. It is an indirect route and pays for proposal-writing, not hardware.
- **Assessment:** For direct hardware or development funding there is nothing open at Catalan level on 18-oct-2026. The realistic Catalan targets are the 2027 editions of the recurring lines covered in Q3.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm the €12,000 cap of the European R+D+I coupon or its eligibility rules for micro-enterprises from an official fact sheet. The PDF was not retrieved.
- I could not see how much budget remains in the open coupon lines; they close early "quan s'exhaureixi el pressupost".

## Q2. Which 2026 calls were relevant but are now CLOSED? (Exact dates, conditions, equipment eligibility)

### Takeaway
The 2026 call that fitted Synkiria best closed on 21-sep-2026: ACCIÓ "Projectes d'exploració tecnològica (R+D+i)". It explicitly lists robotics and AI and "test before invest" PoCs/prototypes, paid 75% up to €30,000, and offered a 60% advance. Its fact sheet lists eligible costs as own personnel, external collaborations, materials and consumables, and audit. It does not list equipment or asset purchase.

The 2026 "Cupons incorporació IA" closed on 30-jul-2026. It covered only an AI diagnosis, excluded equipment, and required more than 10 employees and at least 2 years of age. The other 2026 lines were sector-restricted, required microenterprises to qualify through exceptions, or were for large investments.

### Inferences

But the fact sheet's cost list (personnel / external collaborations / materials-consumables / audit) suggests a ~€5–20k robot would not count as an equipment line. It might be argued as "material" for the prototype, or the "equipament especialitzat" wording in the action list might cover it. Neither reading is verified, and I have not read the article text of the bases.
- **Assessment:** The fit is weak for the AI coupon (more than 10 employees and equipment excluded), the sector R&D lines (automotive or chemical value chain, €120k minimum) and alt impacte (scale).
- **Assessment:** For Noves oportunitats de negoci, the Baix Penedès exception keeps a micro-enterprise eligible for phase 1 (business plan). Phase 2 is the investment phase, and it is reachable only after obtaining phase 1 in a prior call. That makes it a 2-year path at best. Equipment eligibility in phase 2 was not verified.

## Q3. Which recurring calls are likely to reopen (2027), and what other non-cash or support routes exist (DIH4CAT, FEDER, Startup Capital, Departament d'Empresa i Treball)?

### Takeaway
The line most likely to matter again is the R+D+i innovation framework under bases EMT/2403/2026. Those bases are multi-annual and cover exploració tecnològica, recerca aplicada/DT and innovació tecnològica. A 2027 exploració tecnològica call is plausible but not announced. The 2027 coupon call is also likely (it is annual, but the AI coupon needs more than 10 employees). DIH4CAT Test-Before-Invest services are in a funding gap: phase 2 (2026–2029) is "pending European call".

Two lines are not fits:
- Startup Capital is for startups. Its 2026 edition reportedly closed on 30-jul-2026, unverified.
- The Departament d'Empresa i Treball's "Reindustrialització" line targets industrial manufacturers.

### Cited Findings
- The new bases (EMT/2403/2026) cover three project types: (1) exploració tecnològica (examples given: AI, robotics, IoT, cybersecurity…); (2) recerca aplicada i desenvolupament tecnològic, individual or in cooperation with companies or TECNIO; (3) innovació tecnològica, near-market, including automation and digital transformation.
  - Specific costs are defined in each call.
  - De minimis applies in most lines (€300,000 per 3 years).
  - At least 60% of the approved cost must be justified.
  - Three quotes are needed above the contratación-menor limits.
  - The project must be executed in Catalonia.
  - Calls depend on budget.
  - Source (secondary): [aitpa/texfor summary](https://www.aitpa.es/wp-content/uploads/news-97_a18-cat-noves-bases-reguladores-projectes-ridi-2026.pdf)

  - "Projectes d'innovació tecnològica pimes" required at least **2 years** of company existence, was framed under RIS3CAT, had a €5M budget in a related call and required TRL ≥7.
  - The 2025 individual R&D line was under GBER 2023/1315.
  - The FEDER Catalunya 2021-27 programme has €840.5M.
  - The official 2025 fact sheet URLs now return 404 or HTML.
  - Source: [ACCIÓ fitxa innovació tecnològica pimes (2025, URL now dead)](https://www.accio.gencat.cat/web/.content/01_Serveis/innovacio/digital-i-verda/ajuts-innovacio-tecnologica-verda/docs/ACCIO-fitxa-informativa-projectes-innovacio-tecnologica-pimes-cat.pdf)

  - Offers PoCs, prototypes and advanced diagnostics using the infrastructure of technology centres and universities.
