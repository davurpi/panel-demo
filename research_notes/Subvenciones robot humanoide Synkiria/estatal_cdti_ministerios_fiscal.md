## 1. Which state calls are OPEN on 18-oct-2026 and usable by Synkiria?

### Takeaway
Only two state instruments are realistically open today. One is CDTI Proyectos de I+D (PID), which takes applications all year but is a partially non-repayable LOAN, not a grant. The other is AEI Torres Quevedo 2026, which subsidises hiring a PhD; it is open 8–29 Oct 2026 and is a grant. The other relevant grants are closed for 2026: NEOTEC, Misiones, CPP, Kit Digital/Kit Consulting and the Red.es IA business calls. The Red.es IA call that is open now (C012/26-IA) is not for individual SMEs.

## 5. Fiscal incentives: I+D vs innovación tecnológica deduction (art. 35 LIS), robot cost treatment, monetisation (art. 39.2), Informe Motivado, SS bonus

### Takeaway
Under art. 35 Ley 27/2014, I+D gives a 25% deduction on expenses (42% on the excess over the 2-year average, plus 17% on exclusive qualified researcher personnel). It also gives 8% on investment in tangible/intangible fixed assets used exclusively for R&D, so the robot could generate an 8% deduction on its purchase *plus* 25% on its amortization within R&D expenses. Innovación tecnológica gives 12%, and its base does not include fixed-asset investment. Small companies can monetise the credit at a 20% discount (art. 39.2), but this requires an Informe Motivado and other conditions. Subsidies received reduce the deduction base.

### Cited Findings (all from consolidated Ley 27/2014, last update 02/09/2026 — [BOE Ley 27/2014 consolidada](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-12328))
- **Definition of I+D (art. 35.1.a):** "investigación" is original planned inquiry seeking new scientific/technological knowledge. "desarrollo" is applying research results or other scientific knowledge to make new products or design new processes/systems, or to substantially improve existing ones. The definition also includes:
  - "la creación de un primer prototipo no comercializable y los proyectos de demostración inicial o proyectos piloto, siempre que éstos no puedan convertirse o utilizarse para aplicaciones industriales o para su explotación comercial"
  - "la creación, combinación y configuración de software avanzado, mediante nuevos teoremas y algoritmos..."
  - It excludes routine software maintenance and minor updates.
- **Base of I+D (art. 35.1.b):** R&D expenses, "incluidas las amortizaciones de los bienes afectos", plus investments in tangible and intangible fixed assets excluding buildings and land. Expenses must be individualised by project. The base is reduced by subsidies received for these activities that are attributable as income in the period. Investments count as made when the assets are put in working condition.
- **I+D percentages (art. 35.1.c):**
  - 1.º 25% of expenses, or 42% on the excess over the average of the 2 previous years, plus an additional 17% of personnel costs of qualified researchers assigned exclusively to R&D.
  - 2.º 8% of investments in tangible/intangible fixed assets (excluding buildings/land) "siempre que estén afectos exclusivamente a las actividades de investigación y desarrollo". Assets must stay in the company's patrimony until they fulfil their purpose in the R&D, unless their useful life is shorter.
- **Definition of Innovación tecnológica (art. 35.2.a):** an activity whose result is a technological advance in obtaining new products or production processes, or substantial improvements of existing ones. It includes plans, designs, first non-commercialisable prototype and pilot projects.
- **Base of IT (art. 35.2.b):** limited to four concepts: technological diagnosis; industrial design and process engineering; acquisition of advanced technology (patents, licences, know-how, designs, max 1 M€, not from related parties); and ISO 9000/GMP certification (not implementation). It does **not** include fixed-asset investments. The base is reduced by subsidies.
- **IT percentage (art. 35.2.c):** 12%. The 2020–2021 automotive +38 pp temporary increase is expired. No 2026 change to these percentages appears in the consolidated text.
- **Exclusions (art. 35.3):** activities without significant novelty, adapting an existing product to a specific client's requirements, routine quality improvements, production/provision of services, market studies, staff training.
- **Informe motivado (art. 35.4):** issued by the competent ministry or an adscribed body. It is binding on the Tax Administration as to the qualification of activities as I+D or IT. Taxpayers can also request binding consultations and prior valuation agreements (art. 91 LGT).
- **General limits / carryforward (art. 39.1):** unused art. 35 deductions can be applied over 18 years. Combined deductions are capped at 25% of the cuota íntegra (adjusted), or 50% when art. 35/36 deductions from current-year spending exceed 10% of the cuota. New companies can defer the start of the carryforward period until the first year with profits.
- **Monetisation (art. 39.2):** available to entities taxed at the art. 29.1 or 29.6 rate. The company can exclude the art. 35 deductions from the limit and apply them with a **20% discount**. If cuota is insufficient, it can request **payment (abono)** through the IS return. Limits are 1 M€/year for IT and 3 M€/year for I+D+IT combined, at group level. Requirements:
  - (a) at least one year since the end of the tax period in which the deduction arose without it being applied;
  - (b) average headcount (or R&D+IT headcount) not reduced until the end of the period in (c);
  - (c) an amount equal to the deduction reinvested in R&D/IT expenses or exclusively-assigned fixed assets within 24 months;
  - (d) an **Informe Motivado** or prior valuation agreement.

### Inferences
- **Assessment (robot cost):** If the robot is used *exclusively* for an I+D project (e.g. developing new manipulation/vision/LLM-control algorithms), it can generate two deductions. The first is 8% on the acquisition price as fixed-asset investment (35.1.c.2.º). The second is 25% on the annual amortization charged to the project as R&D expense (35.1.b + 35.1.c.1.º). Whether both may be applied simultaneously to the same asset is a common technical question; the law text lists them as separate bases but this was not confirmed against DGT doctrine here (see Gaps). If the project is classified only as **innovación tecnológica**, the robot purchase is **not** in the base. Only design/engineering, diagnosis and technology acquisition expenses count, at 12%.
- **Assessment (classification):** Integrating an off-the-shelf humanoid with commercial LLMs and Odoo risks falling under the exclusions as adaptation/integration without significant novelty. To be I+D, the project needs genuinely new algorithms or software (e.g. novel grounding of LLM commands into manipulation, new perception pipelines), documented by project. Getting an Informe Motivado is strongly advisable and is mandatory for monetisation.
- **Assessment (monetisation):** For a small company with low tax liability, art. 39.2 cash refund (80% of the credit) is the most valuable route, but only after ≥1 year plus processing time. Any grant obtained (e.g. Torres Quevedo) reduces the deduction base.
