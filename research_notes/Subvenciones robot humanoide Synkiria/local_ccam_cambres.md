# Local and commerce grants (CCAM, Diputació de Tarragona, Ajuntament del Vendrell, Consell Comarcal, Cambres, SOC, associations) for Synkiria, S.L.

Reference date: 18 October 2026. Company: Synkiria, S.L., a software and services company (Odoo, automation, AI), turnover of about 290 k€ in 2025, planning a street-level "local comercial" in or near El Vendrell to validate a Unitree R1 EDU humanoid robot.

Labelling: "Cited Findings" are sourced. "Inferences" are my assessment. Anything not verified is listed under "Gaps".

## 1. CCAM (Consorci de Comerç, Artesania i Moda de Catalunya): 2026 calls for commerce, services and crafts

### Takeaway
The 2026 CCAM commerce call (programmes 1 to 6) was open from 15 July to 12 August 2026 and is now CLOSED. Provisional resolutions for programmes 1, 2, 5 and 6 were published on 14 October 2026. The only CCAM line OPEN on 18 October 2026 is **Programme 4, Àmbit 4: transfer of an economic activity from an upper floor or mezzanine to a street-level premises**, open 5 to 30 October 2026, at 75% up to 30,000 €. According to the CCAM presentation, it is open to "any economic activity". It only fits if Synkiria's current premises are an upper floor or mezzanine that it leaves to move to street level. No CCAM line names humanoid robots or robotics. Programme 5 (digital) lists "chatbots, IA, IoT, automatització, tecnologies al punt de venda", but it excludes computers, tablets and similar hardware, and caps the grant at 5,000 €. It is restricted to commerce and "serveis assimilats", and that list does not include IT services.

### Inferences (assessment)
- **Activity code, the main risk.** The "serveis assimilats" list is closed: mechanics, photography, copy shops, repairs, laundries, hairdressers, bars and restaurants, catering, travel agencies. It does not include IT, programming or consulting services (IAE 1st-section group 84x/845, CNAE 62xx). On a strict reading, Synkiria does not qualify for Programme 4 àmbits 1 and 2 or for Programme 5. The only way would be to register a genuine retail activity at the new local, for example retail sale of IT equipment (IAE epígraf 653.x / 659.x, check the exact one) with real retail sales open to the public. A "showroom" for B2B software is not retail. I found no basis for treating a showroom as eligible.
- **Robot.** No CCAM programme lists robots or robotics. Programme 5 mentions "altres tecnologies" at the point of sale and "IA, chatbots, automatització", but it explicitly excludes generic hardware (computers, tablets, cameras, phones). A humanoid robot is a hardware device. Claiming it as "digitalització del punt de venda" would be a stretch with no explicit normative basis. Even if accepted, the cap is 5,000 € (50%). Àmbit 1 (Obertura) lists "obres, interiorisme, instal·lacions, mobiliari, aparadors", not equipment. Àmbit 2 explicitly excludes machinery and furniture. Conclusion: there is no real normative basis to fund the robot through CCAM.
- **Àmbit 4 (OPEN until 30/10/2026, 14:00).** "Qualsevol activitat econòmica" removes the commerce restriction, which makes this the only CCAM line where a software company might be eligible. It requires an existing activity located on an upper floor or mezzanine (entresol) that is moved to a street-level premises. If Synkiria currently operates from an upper-floor office in Catalonia and moves to a street-level local in El Vendrell, it could fit at 75% up to 30,000 €. Which costs are eligible (works, rent, fit-out; equipment unlikely) must be checked in the specific call. Because one objective is to "incrementar el parc d'habitatge", the bases may require the vacated upper floor to be converted to housing or similar. **Act immediately: about 12 days remain.**
- **Recurring.** Programmes 4 and 5 run every year (the CCAM reports 1,939 openings funded since 2020). A 2027 edition is likely around June to August 2027, with an execution period covering calendar year 2027. That is an inference from the pattern, not an announcement.
- **Programme 5 vs. Synkiria as a provider.** Synkiria may also benefit indirectly as an eligible "empresa especialitzada" (≥3 years' digital experience) that executes Programme 5 projects for retail clients, for example Odoo ERP/CRM/AI for shops.

### Gaps
- I could not read the DOGC texts (documentId 1048069, 1049794) or find the specific resolution for Àmbit 4. The EMT/xxxx/2026 resolution codes, the exact article numbers of the bases (Annex 1, the base on beneficiaries and the base on eligible expenses for Programmes 4 and 5) and any IAE annex are NOT verified. Check in the bases: the base defining "comerç" and "serveis" (beneficiaries) and the specific base for Programme 4 Àmbit 4 (requirements on the vacated floor, eligible expenses, years of prior activity, obligation to keep the activity open).
- Àmbit 4 eligible expenses, the minimum years of prior location on an upper floor, any territorial restrictions and the obligation to maintain the activity: not verified.
- Obligations to keep the business open or keep equipment for X years: not found in the summaries. Check the bases.

### Cited Findings

Estimated budget 400,000 €, max 90% financing and max 25,000 € per entity. Deadline: until 4 September 2026 (CLOSED). — [Diputació de Tarragona, Acord Junta de Govern 23-06-2026](https://mot.dipta.cat/serveis/documents/codi-document/ca3adfc4-5a92-4b26-9122-94a68fe989c0)

### Takeaway
El Vendrell's Pla Estratègic de Subvencions 2026–2028 includes "Ajuts a l'emprenedoria, autònoms i pimes locals" and "Promoció del comerç local" as possible lines. I found no specific 2026 call or bases (BOPT) for opening new establishments, digitalisation or equipment. Recent precedents found are only Covid-era aid from 2020–2021.

### Cited Findings
- The Pla estratègic de subvencions 2026–2028 (Ajuntament del Vendrell, ref. GALC2026000003) lists among its subsidisable subjects (Art. 8) "Ajuts a l'emprenedoria, comerç local i ocupació". Under objective 9.2, "Fomentar el comerç local, l'emprenedoria i l'ocupació de qualitat", it lists programmes 9.2.1 "Promoció del comerç local, la dinamització comercial i campanyes comercials", 9.2.2 "Ajuts a l'emprenedoria, autònoms i pimes locals", 9.2.3 "Foment de l'associacionisme comercial" and 9.2.4 "Millora de l'ocupabilitat… i el desenvolupament econòmic a la comarca". Art. 10: an annual call exists only when there is budget credit and approved bases. The plan is programmatic and "no crea drets ni obligacions" for individuals. — [Ajuntament del Vendrell, Pla Estratègic de Subvencions 2026-2028](https://seu-e.cat/documents/2000497/15504537/Pla+Estrat%C3%A8gic+Subvencions+2026-2028/365de0fb-3be3-4a4b-a19f-536efbea3488)

- Tax bonuses: the published list of El Vendrell's 2026 fiscal-ordinance changes covers IBI (OF 2), parking (OF 18) and water and sewerage (OF 9 and 10). - Line 9.2.2 means a call for local SMEs is possible in 2026–2028, but no call has been verified. Given the size of past municipal lines, any aid would probably be small (hundreds to a few thousand €).
- ICIO bonuses (up to 95% for works of "especial interès o utilitat municipal", including job creation, under art. 103.2.a TRLRHL) and IAE/IBI bonuses can exist in municipal ordinances. Check El Vendrell's Ordenança Fiscal on ICIO before any works licence. These are tax savings, not grants.

## 5. Cambres de Comerç (Tarragona, and the Pyme Digital / Pyme Sostenible programmes)

### Takeaway
The Cambra de Tarragona's **Pyme Digital 2026** (BDNS 889473) gives 40% FEDER aid on up to 7,490 € of implementation (max 2,996 €). Applications closed on 31 July 2026 or earlier if the budget ran out, so it is now CLOSED, and the budget was tiny (44,940 € in aid, about 15 companies). The sister programme Pyme Sostenible excludes plans "coincidentes con la actividad de la empresa", a clause that may affect a software company. Hardware eligibility was not verified.

### Cited Findings
- Pyme Digital 2026, Cambra de Tarragona: BDNS 889473, BOPT 02-03-2026 (CVE 2026-01687). Beneficiaries: "Pymes, de la demarcación territorial de la Cámara de Comercio de Tarragona, que se encuentren dadas de alta en el Censo del IAE". It offers aid "para implantar soluciones TIC, subvencionadas en un 40%" by FEDER POPE 2021-2027. Total aid 44,940 € on a 112,350 € budget for Phase II. "La cuantía máxima de ayuda por empresa será de 2.996,00 sobre un presupuesto máximo elegible de 7.490,00 € (7.000,00 € de coste directo + 490,00 € de costes indirectos)", prefinanced by the company. The diagnosis and follow-up services are free. Applications ran until 14:00 on 31 July 2026 or until the budget was exhausted. — [BOPT, anunci 356936 (PDF)](https://www.dipta.cat/bopt/web/anunci/descarrega-pdf/356936); [CIDO Diba fitxa](https://cido.diba.cat/subvencions/21170143/ajuts-del-programa-pyme-digital-2026-cambra-oficial-de-comerc-industria-i-navegacio-de-tarragona); [BDNS 889473](https://www.infosubvenciones.es/bdnstrans/GE/es/convocatorias/889473)
- Pyme Sostenible 2026, Cambra de Tarragona (convocatoria of 25-02-2026), same structure: 40% FEDER, max 2,996 €.
  - Requirements: an SME registered in the IAE "antes del 1 de enero de 2024"; "facturación mínima de 100.000 € en la última anualidad"; registered office or production centre in the Cambra de Tarragona demarcation; de minimis Reg. (UE) 2023/2831.
  - Clause on own activity: "el Plan de Implantación no podrá incluir proyectos coincidentes con la actividad de la empresa, aunque su ejecución se contrate a terceros".
  - Prohibition on linked suppliers (art. 68.2 RD 887/2006).
  - Applications ran until 31/07/2026.

  — [Cámara de Tarragona sede, convocatoria Pyme Sostenible 2026 (PDF)](https://sede.camara.es/sede/tarragona/tramites/TR0000006409/documento/002900068256)

### Inferences (assessment)
- These programmes are CLOSED for 2026 and recur every year. A new edition is likely around February or March 2027 with first-come-first-served admission, so applying early matters. That is an inference from the pattern.
- Synkiria meets the size and turnover thresholds (>100 k€). If Pyme Digital has the same "actividad coincidente" clause as Pyme Sostenible (likely, since both are run by the Cámara de España, but not verified), a software and AI integration company could not fund a software or AI implementation that matches its own activity. Even if a robot counted as a "solución TIC", the maximum aid is 2,996 €.
- Whether El Vendrell (Baix Penedès) falls within the Cambra de Tarragona's demarcation is not confirmed. Under the 2026 calls, eligibility depends on "domicilio social y/o centro productivo" being in the demarcation.

### Gaps
- Annex II "Tipología y Justificación de Gastos elegibles" of Pyme Digital: not accessed, so hardware and equipment eligibility is unverified.
- The Cambra de Tarragona demarcation's coverage of El Vendrell, and whether the Cambres of Valls or Reus or Cambres Catalunya run separate 2026 digitalisation programmes (e.g., Indústria 4.0, ciberseguretat): not verified.
